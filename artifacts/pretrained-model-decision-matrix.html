Project Overview

This artifact compares pre-trained models from NLP/Generative AI, Computer Vision, and tabular-data domains. It highlights practical trade-offs between model size, speed, accuracy, and explainability to support better model selection decisions.

Course:AIML-501: Model Development
Artifact Type:Decision Matrix (Web)
Focus:Size vs Accuracy vs Speed

Introduction and Methodology

I selected six commonly used models across three domains: BERT-base and LLaMA-7B (NLP/GenAI), MobileNetV3 and EfficientNet-B0 (Vision), and XGBoost plus Random Forest (Tabular). I compared approximate model size, relative speed, benchmark-level performance patterns, and explainability considerations.

Decision Matrix

Model Domain Model Size Accuracy Potential Inference Speed Explainability Best-fit Scenario
BERT-base NLP ~110M params High Medium Moderate Text classification, NER, document understanding
LLaMA-7B Generative AI ~7B params High Lower Limited General generation, chat, summarization
MobileNetV3 Vision Small footprint Good Fast Moderate Mobile/edge image classification
EfficientNet-B0 Vision Moderate size High Medium Moderate Balanced cloud or production CV pipelines
XGBoost Tabular Tree ensemble High Fast Strong (SHAP) Structured data, risk scoring, forecasting
Random Forest Tabular Tree ensemble Good Medium Strong Baseline tabular tasks with interpretability
Note: Values are comparative and intended for decision support, not single-dataset absolute rankings.

Analysis and Recommendations

  • Choose larger NLP/GenAI models when quality and breadth matter more than latency and cost.
  • For real-time or edge vision workloads, MobileNet-style models usually provide the best speed trade-off.
  • For tabular business data, XGBoost often gives strong performance with practical explainability support.
  • Use Random Forest when you need robust baselines and easier communication with stakeholders.
