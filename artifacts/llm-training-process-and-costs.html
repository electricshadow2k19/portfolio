End-to-End LLM Training Pipeline

This pipeline shows the main steps from data collection to deployment. Each step has a distinct cost profile and introduces different technical and governance requirements.

Step 1

Data acquisition & governance

Collect sources and manage rights, privacy, and safety constraints.

  • Web crawl + licensed datasets
  • PII filtering & compliance
  • Data documentation
Step 2

Cleaning, dedupe & tokenization

Turn raw text into high-quality training tokens.

  • Quality filtering & deduplication
  • Tokenizer build + vocab
  • Train/val/test splits
Step 3

Pre-training at scale

Compute-heavy step: optimize billions of parameters over massive token counts.

  • Distributed training (data/model parallel)
  • Checkpointing & failure recovery
  • Evaluation during training
Step 4

Alignment & safety tuning

Improve helpfulness, reduce harmful outputs, and set behavioral constraints.

  • Instruction tuning / preference learning
  • Safety policy + red teaming
  • Monitoring + evaluation suites

What affects cost the most?

Training cost scales with (a) tokens processed, (b) model size, and (c) training efficiency (hardware utilization, parallelism, and stability).

Practical constraints include checkpoint storage, network bandwidth between GPUs, failures/restarts, and staffing to run 24/7 operations during large training runs.

Tip for readers: A single “training cost” number can be misleading. The same target model can cost drastically different amounts depending on hardware prices, utilization, and whether training must be repeated.

Why Costs Differ Across GPT, Claude, Gemini, LLaMA

1

Scale targets

More parameters and more training tokens usually improve capability, but increase compute, energy, and time.

2

Efficiency and hardware choices

Utilization, parallelism strategy, and accelerator type (GPU/TPU) can change cost dramatically.

3

Alignment, evaluation, and iteration

Safety tuning, red teaming, and repeated training runs add significant time and staffing costs beyond pre-training.
