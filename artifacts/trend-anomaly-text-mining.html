Analysis Pipeline

  • 1. Load & detect columns — Auto-identify narrative text and date fields in the CFPB dataset.
  • 2. Preprocess text — Tokenize, remove stopwords, normalize complaint narratives (NLTK).
  • 3. Engineer features — Word count, character count, unique-word ratio per complaint.
  • 4. Detect anomalies — Isolation Forest (~5% contamination) flags structurally unusual narratives.
  • 5. Analyze trends — Monthly aggregates of volume, average text length, and anomaly counts.
  • 6. Export results — Excel workbooks and PNG visualizations for review and reporting.

Ethics & Responsible Use

Anomaly flags reflect statistical outliers in text-length features—not proof of misconduct. Results should be reviewed by humans before operational decisions. Length-based metrics may correlate with writing style or literacy; apply the Transparency, Accountability, and Fairness (TAF) framework when communicating findings to stakeholders.
