Trend & Anomaly Detection on Consumer Complaints - Explanatory Document (Portfolio Artifact)
Arun Kumar Vallala | DTAN-520: Text Mining | Indiana Wesleyan University

Purpose
This artifact demonstrates an end-to-end text mining workflow applied to Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) complaint narratives. It combines NLP preprocessing, statistical feature engineering, unsupervised anomaly detection, and time-based trend analysis. The walkthrough video (arun_trend_analysis.mp4) explains the pipeline, results, and ethical considerations for non-technical audiences.

1) Problem & data
- Domain: Consumer financial complaints (cleaned CFPB dataset).
- Goal: Flag unusually structured complaint narratives and track how complaint text length changes over time.
- Inputs: Narrative text and date-received fields (auto-detected when column names vary).

2) Text preprocessing
- Lowercasing, punctuation removal, tokenization (NLTK).
- Stopword removal and short-token filtering to focus on meaningful terms.

3) Feature engineering
- word_count: tokens per complaint
- char_count: raw narrative length
- unique_word_ratio: lexical diversity (unique tokens / word count)

4) Anomaly detection
- Algorithm: Isolation Forest (scikit-learn), contamination ≈ 5%.
- Output: Normal vs. Anomaly labels based on multivariate text-length features.
- Interpretation: Anomalies are structurally unusual narratives (very short/long, atypical vocabulary spread)—not necessarily fraudulent, but worth review.

5) Trend analysis
- Monthly aggregation of record counts, average word/character counts, and anomaly counts.
- Visualization: line chart of average word count over time; scatter plot of char_count vs. word_count with anomaly highlighting.

6) Deliverables
- Processed Excel workbook (full data, anomalies sheet, trend sheet).
- Anomaly-only export for focused review.
- Python script: arun_trend_anomaly_analysis.py
- Video demo: media/arun_trend_analysis.mp4

7) Ethics & responsible use (aligned with course TAF framework)
- Transparency: Document preprocessing and model assumptions; anomalies are statistical flags, not accusations.
- Accountability: Human review should validate flagged cases before action.
- Fairness: Length-based features may correlate with language complexity or consumer literacy—avoid punitive use without context.

Summary
This project shows practical text mining skills—cleaning, feature design, unsupervised learning, and temporal analysis—on real regulatory text data, with clear communication via video for portfolio and professional audiences.
